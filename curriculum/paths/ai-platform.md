# AI Platform Engineer path

For platform and infrastructure engineers who are asked to run models, not train them. The path takes the container and Kubernetes primitives you need (isolation, images and registries, the reconciler, the scheduler, Services and the Gateway API) and goes straight to the parts that are different for AI workloads: GPUs as a scarce and divisible resource, model servers as batch schedulers with a KV cache, gateways that count tokens, autoscaling on queue depth, prefix-aware routing, vector stores, models as supply-chain artifacts, and what a token really costs.

Lessons appear here as they are published; prerequisites are listed on each lesson page.
