# Site Reliability Engineer path

For engineers who keep production services up and want to know what their tools are doing underneath. The path builds a time-series database, a PromQL engine, an alert router, a log pipeline and a tracer, then turns them into SLOs with burn-rate alerts, an incident process and chaos experiments with a hypothesis. It starts from the Linux, network and Kubernetes internals that every one of those signals comes from, and ends with scheduled jobs, safe rollouts, DORA metrics and a triage agent.

Lessons appear here as they are published; prerequisites are listed on each lesson page.
