# Sponsoring CloudCaptain

CloudCaptain is free and stays free: every lesson, test, quiz, skill and reference page is MIT-licensed, with no paywall, no email gate and no premium tier. Sponsorship pays for the time it takes to keep it that way and keep it current.

## What your support pays for

- **New lessons.** Each lesson is a production problem, a tool built from scratch with tests, the same job with the real tool, a quiz and an artifact, then a smoke run against real Docker, Kubernetes, OpenTofu and cloud emulators before it is marked verified. The [roadmap](./ROADMAP.md) has nine phases still to write.
- **Keeping it true.** Tools move every month. The weekly version refresh, the deprecation scan and the smoke runs catch drift; fixing what they find is ongoing work.
- **The free tier staying free.** CI minutes, the domain and the preview deployments for every pull request.

## Ways to support

| Tier | How | What you get |
|:--|:--|:--|
| ☕ **Supporter** | [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/nomadicmehul), one-off | Thanks, and the knowledge that it keeps the content free |
| 🚀 **Backer** | [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/nomadicmehul), monthly | Your name in the backers list below and priority on issues you open |
| 🏢 **Organisation** | [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/nomadicmehul) or get in touch via [nomadicmehul.dev](https://nomadicmehul.dev/) | A conversation about the lessons your teams need; logo placement by agreement |

Not in a position to pay? A ⭐ [star](https://github.com/nomadicmehul/CloudCaptain), a share, a fixed typo, a translation or a new lesson helps just as much. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Backers and sponsors

GitHub keeps the up-to-date list on the [sponsors page](https://github.com/sponsors/nomadicmehul). Backers and organisations who would like to be named here are added with each release.
